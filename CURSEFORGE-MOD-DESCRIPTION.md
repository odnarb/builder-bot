# 🤖 Minecraft AI Builder (Free Tier)

**Build with AI-powered commands inside Minecraft!**  
This mod brings your ideas to life through intelligent structure generation — just type what you want to build, and the AI translates your prompt into blocks.

---

## 🆓 Free Tier Features
- ✅ Use AI to generate builds from natural language (e.g., “build a stone house with windows”)
- ✅ Supports **up to 50 blocks** per build
- ✅ WebbUI & In-game chat-driven command system
- ✅ Works in **singleplayer and multiplayer**
- ✅ No account or payment required

---

## 🚀 Want to Build Bigger?
Unlock premium features with a subscription:

## 📦 Installation
1. Requires [Fabric/Forge/Quilt] + any required dependencies
2. Drop this mod into your `mods` folder
3. Launch Minecraft and type `!build cube house` to begin!
